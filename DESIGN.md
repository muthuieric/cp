# Karan Holdings & Architectural Real Estate Design Specification

## Overview
A modern, brutalist-editorial design system created for luxury commercial real estate, corporate headquarters, and architectural developments in Kenya. The interface moves away from consumer residential templates into austere, museum-grade architectural spatial clarity.

## Visual Principles
1. **Austere Minimalism:** High-contrast monochromatic foundations (pure white, deep architectural charcoal, warm stone surfaces).
2. **Editorial Monospaced & Tracked Labels:** All section markers, badges, and navigation links feature uppercase wide tracking (`letter-spacing: 0.2em - 0.25em`).
3. **Underlined Anchor Tags:** Key section identifiers (e.g. `About Us`, `Services`, `Curated Works`) utilize clean, single-pixel black bottom borders rather than pill backgrounds.
4. **Hero Architecture Integration:** The hero is an architectural volume — full-bleed dusk modernist villa photography with white editorial typography and an embedded bottom statistics bar separated by hairline vertical dividers.
5. **Commercial Grade Information Architecture:** Tailored for corporate clients, commercial developments, Grade A office spaces, institutional landlords, and commercial retail hubs.

## Components
- **Navbar:** Clean architectural bar with geometric emblem mark, uppercase tracked links (`STUDIO`, `SERVICES`, `PROJECTS`, `INSIGHTS`, `CONTACT`, `FAQ`), and social action links.
- **Hero Banner:** Editorial headline (*Where Vision Meets Structure, And Design BecomesPurpose.*), `Discover now →` link, and integrated stats card.
- **About Us Section:** Side-by-side architectural mission (*Innovative Architecture for a Changing World*) and 3-card project ribbon.
- **Commercial Solutions:** Highlighting spaces tailored for business efficiency, brand prestige, and commercial yields.
- **Property Showcase:** Minimalist cards with architectural photography, commercial classification badges (`GRADE A`, `COMMERCIAL`, `RETAIL`), square footage, and lease/sale terms.
